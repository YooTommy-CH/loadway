# Loadway

Official downloads for Loadway, a free native macOS app for saving MP4 video and MP3 audio from supported links.

**Current release: [Loadway 1.0.1](https://github.com/YooTommy-CH/loadway/releases/tag/v1.0.1)**

[Download for macOS](https://github.com/YooTommy-CH/loadway/releases/download/v1.0.1/Loadway-1.0.1-macOS.dmg)

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Open the DMG and drag Loadway into Applications. Existing Sparkle-enabled installations can use **Check for Updates** in Loadway.

Version 1.0.1 adds a movable quick bar with remembered position, saved quick-download quality preferences, optional encrypted queue restoration, optional completion notifications, clearer errors, and updated components. English and Italian. No accounts, advertising or analytics.

The matching FFmpeg 7.1.5 and LAME 3.100 sources, licenses and build instructions are available in [Loadway-1.0.1-FFmpeg-source.zip](https://github.com/YooTommy-CH/loadway/releases/download/v1.0.1/Loadway-1.0.1-FFmpeg-source.zip). Checksums are attached to the release.

Site support depends on the source. Loadway does not bypass DRM or restricted access. Source websites receive normal network requests, including your IP address.
